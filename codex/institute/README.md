# Hermeticon Institute — Zürich in a bauble

## World contract · 2026-10-23

**This is the institute, not a website about the institute.** No sales pitch, recruitment copy, or institutional façade. The public entrance opens directly into a beautiful living world inside the existing Hermeticon glass-and-brass vessel. Zürich is the institute's chosen location and artistic setting, not a requirement for a geospatially accurate city model.

### Inheritance

- **Genesis** supplies the existing signed terrain, vessel, botanical and creature populations, Wanderer, meteors, detached bead physics, observation lens, and measured ownership accounting. Preserve its accepted HYBRID visual treatment and controls.
- **Evermore II** supplies the ecological design language: interacting populations, needs, resource transport, predation, construction, opportunistic labor, death and replenishment. Port behavior through explicit interfaces; do not paste its entire world or substitute decorative animation for ecology.
- **Athanor** remains the host. **Arcana** owns reusable mechanisms. **Codex** composes this world. No god object.

### The perpetual cycle

1. **Flourish:** autonomous organisms cultivate, gather, build, consume, wander, and form local structures. A playful, compact, Swiss/Alpine-inspired miniature emerges without explanatory copy.
2. **Cinderfall:** one tap launches a meteor. Impact deforms signed terrain and releases authored matter into the existing detached inventory. Structures break into recoverable components; creatures can die; populations can respond.
3. **Aftermath:** material persists with identity and provenance. Rubble and bodies are not merely particles to fade out. Ecology encounters the changed topology and inventory.
4. **Recovery:** scavengers and builders claim recoverable matter, transport it, and restore habitats and structures according to local conditions. Regrowth consumes resources or reclaims existing authored matter; it cannot silently mint replacements.
5. **Reformation:** the world finds a new equilibrium, not necessarily the exact pre-impact arrangement. Repeated meteors are allowed during recovery; no forced reset timer.

### Material accounting — hard invariant

Every accounting unit has exactly one current owner/state at any instant. Initial world matter plus explicitly introduced matter equals current authored/attached matter + detached matter + inventories + transported matter + deposited matter + explicitly exported matter. Transfers are atomic; never decrement one side without incrementing another. The existing Genesis BB accounting remains authoritative until a unified ledger is proved equivalent. Terrain excavation must account for its own units, distinct from botanical BBs; do not falsely claim all signed-field volume is already in the BB ledger.

Each observation snapshot should report: baseline, introduced, exported, authored, detached, held, in transit, deposited, and discrepancy, along with births, deaths, impacts, repairs and recovery progress. **A zero discrepancy is a test result, not an assumption.**

### Design principles

- Delight before dashboards. Readable motion, tiny purposeful agents, satisfying impact and visibly consequential repair.
- Destruction can be total locally; recovery remains possible if sufficient matter and viable agents survive. If not, a visible, explicitly accounted seed/recolonization path is needed. Never fake infinite free regeneration.
- Meteor interaction stays immediate, repeatable, and mobile-friendly.
- Preserve existing Hermeticon appearance, navigation, accepted populations, and material behavior until a replacement is explicitly observed and accepted.
- No fixed scripted diorama or mandatory cinematic. The ecology runs autonomously.
- No sales pitch. No careers, résumé, or corporate copy in the world.

### First executable vertical slice

A single small recoverable structure assembled from accounted units, one local carrier/builder behavior, and an impact-to-rubble-to-repair loop, mounted as an **optional** Genesis subsystem. Make the units visible and observable. Verify ledger balance before and after repeated impacts and partial repairs. Only then extend to ecology-wide building and restoration. Add an accessible but unobtrusive world inspection readout to the existing 👁 instrument; no new permanent panel.

### Publication discipline

Existing live Hermeticon is a protected baseline. Implement the slice in a separate composable module, test it, mount carefully, then publish through the existing Pages workflow and verify the actual live build. Do not confuse a spec commit with a shipped world.
